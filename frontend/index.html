How it works
A frozen linear probe (fit during prior research, no retraining tonight) reads one hidden-state row of the VLA on the exact frame it acts from. The same forward pass yields the policy's planned action. The safety layer is that probe run as a GO/HOLD monitor: HOLD zeroes the motion command. Belief and plan disagreeing on the shallow carton is the knowledge-action gap.
